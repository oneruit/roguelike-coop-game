## Mandatory Negative Constraints
No anime style, no kawaii style, no cute expression, no childish appearance, no soft pastel colors, no realistic proportions, no photorealism, no smooth gradients.

Create a subtle, natural, **seamless 6-frame idle animation** for the same character in all four directional rows. The character remains in a stable combat-ready standing pose with **chibi proportions (2.5 to 3 heads tall)** and a grim, serious expression. This is not walking, running, attacking, or changing position.

## Frame sequence, left to right

1. Neutral alert idle pose (chibi proportions, sturdy stance, battle glare).
2. Small inhale; subtle chest and shoulder movement.
3. Peak of the breathing motion; a few hair, cloth, strap or feather pixels shift naturally.
4. Gentle exhale; shoulders settle. A brief blink is allowed only in views where eyes are visible.
5. Eyes reopen where applicable; secondary motion settles.
6. Return to approximately the same pose as frame 1 so the loop is seamless.

Keep motion restrained and believable. Use small pixel-level changes in breathing, hair, cloak, loose fabric, accessories, or weapon position. Do not move the root position or slide the feet. Keep the weapon in the same hands with a stable, plausible grip. Every frame must be a distinct phase, but changes should remain subtle. Maintain the 2.5–3 heads tall chibi proportions consistently in every single frame.

Apply the same sequence timing to all rows. BACK must not show the face or eyes. LEFT remains strictly left-facing; RIGHT remains strictly right-facing throughout all six frames. Keep camera projection, scale, silhouette and baseline consistent. No large head turns or pose changes.

Output exactly 6 columns × 4 rows, 128×128 px per cell, 768×512 px total, pure white background. No labels, grid lines, extra frames, scenery, shadows, or effects. The character and weapon must stay completely inside each cell.
