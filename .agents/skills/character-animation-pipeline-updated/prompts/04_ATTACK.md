## Mandatory Negative Constraints
No anime style, no kawaii style, no cute expression, no childish appearance, no soft pastel colors, no realistic proportions, no photorealism, no smooth gradients.

Create a six-frame melee **one-shot attack animation** using the character's existing weapon. Preserve **chibi proportions (2.5 to 3 heads tall)** and perform one controlled, powerful strike appropriate to the weapon and character. This is not a loop, not a walk attack, and not a special ability unless the character's approved design explicitly makes that their normal physical strike.

## Frame sequence, left to right

1. Ready stance with a stable chibi combat posture and correct weapon grip.
2. Preparation: weight shifts; the weapon starts moving into position.
3. Wind-up: sturdy arms, shoulders and torso prepare the strike; the weapon follows a coherent trajectory.
4. Main strike: the strongest, clearest attack pose and main weapon extension.
5. Follow-through: body and weapon continue naturally along the physical trajectory.
6. Recovery: the character regains a stable stance suitable for transitioning back to IDLE.

The six poses must be distinct and temporally ordered. Use the same attack concept and timing in all four rows, adapted to each view:
- FRONT shows the same strike from the front.
- BACK shows it from behind.
- LEFT shows it from a strict left profile.
- RIGHT shows it from a strict right profile.

The weapon remains the exact same item in every frame. Preserve length, blade/head shape, material and color; keep its shaft connected to the blade/head. Hands must maintain plausible contact and grip. Do not make the weapon teleport, stretch, disappear, duplicate or change hands unexpectedly. Do not turn the character toward the camera in side rows. Keep the 2.5–3 heads tall chibi proportions stable across all 6 frames.

**Absolutely no visual effects:** no anime slash arcs, glowing blade, magic, particles, sparks, impact flashes, motion trails, speed lines, dust clouds or extra effects. Communicate the attack solely with the character's body, hands and the physical position of the weapon.

Fit the entire character, weapon tip, blade/head, hands, and limbs inside each 128×128 cell. Do not crop the weapon to fit; simplify the pose or orient the weapon diagonally while keeping the same attack readable. Preserve the same scale, art style and camera projection throughout the sheet.

Output exactly 6 columns × 4 rows, 128×128 px per cell, 768×512 px total, pure white background. No labels, grid lines, extra frames, scenery, shadows or visual effects.
