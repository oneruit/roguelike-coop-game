# Character Turnaround — create and approve before animation

Attach the original character image, reference `references/chibi_style_reference.png`, and paste the completed `00_CHARACTER_LOCK.md` design details. Create a **static reference turnaround, not an animation sheet**.

## Mandatory Negative Constraints
No anime style, no kawaii style, no cute expression, no childish appearance, no soft pastel colors, no realistic proportions, no photorealism, no smooth gradients.

Show exactly four full-body views of the same character, arranged left to right in one clean row:

1. FRONT — front view.
2. BACK — direct rear view.
3. LEFT — strict left-facing profile.
4. RIGHT — strict right-facing profile.

## Anatomical & Styling Requirements
- **Chibi proportions**: Strict 2.5 to 3 heads tall ratio. Large readable head (~30–35% of height), compact sturdy torso, and thick boots with grounded stance. Exaggerated, readable weapon silhouette. No realistic 7–8 head human proportions.
- **Maturity & Face**: The character is an experienced, serious, battle-ready adult. Sharp jaw, sharp small-to-medium eyes with intense glare, scars/beards/stubble where appropriate. No anime eyes, no kawaii blush, no cute baby face.
- **Consistency**: Use one neutral combat-ready standing pose in all four views. The feet share the same baseline (approx y=118), and the character has the same scale and proportions in every view. Keep the same weapon, equipment, clothing, armor, hair and distinctive asymmetric details. Depict unseen areas logically and consistently instead of inventing new decoration. Show the actual back of the hood/cloak/armor in BACK and the correct side silhouette in LEFT and RIGHT.
- **Perspective**: Crisp 16-bit pixel-art style and 2.5D game-sprite perspective compatible with a camera tilted approximately 30° downward. Directional views of a 2D sprite character, not a rendered 3D model. LEFT and RIGHT are strict profile views, not three-quarter views.
- **Canvas & Background**: Pure solid white background RGB(255,255,255). No text, view labels, grid, borders, shadows, scenery, VFX or extra equipment. Keep the views clearly separated and complete.

**Do not animate. Do not create a 6×4 grid. Do not create multiple poses per view. Do not proceed to IDLE/WALK/ATTACK until the human approves this turnaround.**
